## 4. "I bought this" on saved items
On the Saved page (and in the listing sheet when the item is saved), add an "I bought it" action. It should:
- ask for the price I paid, pre-filled with the current listing price in £ and editable;
- copy the listing's first photo and title into my **Wardrobe** as a new taste item, so it is embedded and starts shaping the feed, and record where it came from: source, listing URL, brand, size and purchase price;
- move the item out of Saved into the wardrobe;
- stop price-drop alerts for it.

Undo must be possible: removing it from the wardrobe should not delete the original listing data.

## 5. Wardrobe value, one step at a time
I want to see what my wardrobe is worth. Build this in stages and stop after each stage for my feedback.
- **5a. Prices I enter.** Each wardrobe item gets an optional purchase price and an optional custom value I can type in. Show a total at the top of the Wardrobe page: what I paid in total and what it's worth now. Items bought through feature 4 arrive with their price already filled in.
- **5b. Estimated resale value.** For each item, estimate the current resale value from comparable listings Rack has already collected: same brand plus visual similarity (CLIP) plus category, using the median £ price of the closest matches. - **5c.
