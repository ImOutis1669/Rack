## How it scores today
`score = Σ wᵢ·cᵢ / Σ wᵢ` over the parts that exist (looks, text, votes, deal, fit), each 0-1,
`+ brand · affinity` (love 1.0, like 0.6, curious 0.3; unknown designers add 0)
`− knockoff_penalty · knock`. Default weights: looks 0.40, text 0.10, votes ("feedback") 0.15,
deal 0.10, fit 0.10, brand 0.15, knock-off 0.50. They're per user, stored in `profile.data`
(`weights`), and editable under Settings > Advanced: score weights (web).

The weighted parts add up to 0.85, not 1, so votes are really 0.15 / 0.85 = 17.6% of the average
and looks 47%.

What my top 60 looked like on 17 November (average per part): designer 0.94, votes 0.83, fit
0.78, text 0.72, deal 0.62, looks 0.49. The top is mostly "my loved designers again"; the
designer bonus sits on top of the average, so a loved designer with average looks beats an
unknown designer that looks right.

My votes are 99 upvotes, 5 downvotes and 28 saves (votes and saves both feed the Rocchio vector,
blended with my 14 wardrobe/wishlist photos).
